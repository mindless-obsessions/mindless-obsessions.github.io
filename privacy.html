Privacy Policy

Effective November 12, 2026

Summary

Mindless Obsessions Publisher is an internal tool that publishes videos to our own official social accounts. It does not collect data from the public, does not sell data, and does not use data for advertising.

What the tool accesses

  • TikTok (Login Kit, Content Posting API): our own account's basic profile (display name, avatar, open ID) and permission to upload videos to our own account or drafts.
  • YouTube (YouTube Data API): our own channel's basic information and permission to upload videos to our own channel.
  • Instagram / Facebook (Meta APIs): our own professional account and Page, to publish Reels.
  • Public performance numbers for our own posts (views, likes, comments, shares) to measure what works.

How it is stored

Access tokens are stored only on our own computer in the operating system's encrypted keyring. They are never published, shared, or included in backups. Video files and statistics stay on our computer.

Sharing

We do not sell or share personal data with third parties. Data is sent only to the platform being published to, as required to publish.

Website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages and does not use cookies or analytics of its own.
