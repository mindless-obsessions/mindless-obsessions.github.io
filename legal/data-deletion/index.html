Data deletion requests

Last updated 2026-11-12

You can ask us to delete information we hold about you, including creator research records, permission records, or emails.

How to request

  1. Email mindlessobsessions007@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion request".
  2. Tell us what to delete (for example your channel link or the email address you contacted us from).
  3. We may ask a question to confirm the request comes from you.

What happens

We delete the information from our records, generally within 30 days, and confirm by email. If we must keep a minimal record (for example that a creator asked us never to feature their content or contact them again), we keep only what's needed to honor that request.

Platform data

Information you shared on TikTok, Instagram, YouTube or Discord is controlled by those platforms; use their settings to delete it. Data in our Roblox games is handled through Roblox's systems and policies.
