Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026-11-12

This policy explains what information Mindless Obsessions ("we", "us") handles, why, and your choices. It covers this website, our official social accounts, our Discord server, and the internal tools we use to publish videos to our own accounts.

This website

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. We use no cookies, analytics, ads, trackers or forms. GitHub, as our host, may process technical data such as IP addresses in server logs to deliver and secure the site; see GitHub's privacy statement.

When you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address and message and use them only to reply and handle your request. We keep correspondence only as long as needed for that purpose, including records of creator permissions.

Roblox creators we research or contact

To find creators whose clips we might feature with permission, we keep a private list of public creator channel information: channel name, links, public statistics, recent public video titles, and any business contact the creator publishes. We use it only to request permission, keep permission records, and credit creators. We do not sell it. Creators can ask us to delete their information or not to contact them; see Data deletion.

Social media and Discord

When you interact with our TikTok, Instagram, YouTube or Discord, those platforms' own privacy policies apply. We may see your public username and public interactions, and we use aggregate statistics about our own posts to understand what works.

Our publishing tools

Our internal tool "Mindless Obsessions Publisher" connects to our own accounts through official platform APIs (TikTok, YouTube, Meta) only to upload our videos and read statistics for our own posts. Access tokens are stored on our own computer in the operating system's encrypted keyring and are never published or shared.

Sharing

We do not sell or rent personal information and do not share it for advertising. We share information only with the platforms needed to publish our content, or where the law requires.

Children

This website is a general-audience site about Roblox games and entertainment. It collects no personal information through forms or tracking. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13; if you believe a child has emailed us, contact us and we will delete it. Our Roblox games run on Roblox and are subject to Roblox's own privacy policy and parental controls.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us what information we have about you, to correct it, or to delete it. See Data deletion. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local law.

Changes

We will update this page when our practices change and change the date above.
